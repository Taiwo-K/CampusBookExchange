#CampusBookExchange
A student to student course material marketplace and budget planner for the University Of North Texas.
UNT students can sell, buy, or give away textbooks and course materials they no longer need, and other students find them by course number or name. The budget planner estimates what a semester's required materials cost at retail, then shows what those same materials cost from other students.
